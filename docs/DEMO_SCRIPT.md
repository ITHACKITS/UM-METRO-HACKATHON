# 3-minute demo script

Before the demo: run the app once on the demo field (caches the satellite read). If venue Wi-Fi is doubtful, start the API with `JAGAPADI_OFFLINE=1`; the recorded responses are real and show their capture time.

**0:00 – Problem (25 s).** Landing page. "Last year's El Niño cut Indonesia's rice harvest area by 2.29%. Farmers learn about drought when the soil cracks." Click **Kering** on the dock: the paddies dry and crack. Click **Banjir**: muddy water rises. Back to **Normal**.

**0:25 – Story (20 s).** Scroll once through the chapters: the diorama acts out drought, the top-down planting view, a flood, dusk. "Everything you see is generated in the browser."

**0:45 – Draw and analyse (35 s).** **Buka peta** → **Pakai lahan contoh**. While it loads: "We read this field's own Sentinel-2 history, 51 ECMWF rain scenarios, GloFAS river flow and the terrain." Point at the gauges, the scene age and confidence chips.

**1:20 – Drought (30 s).** Kering tab: first-stress day with its P5–P95 range, the daily probability strip, "Karena" drivers, then the actions ranked from easiest. Press play on the timeline: the field turns gold, then orange.

**1:50 – Scenario (25 s).** **Uji skenario**: rain ×0.3, delay 10 days → run. The banner says HIPOTETIS. "This is planning, not a forecast."

**2:15 – Ask Si Padi (20 s).** "Apakah sawah saya akan kekeringan?" The answer quotes the same numbers and names its sources.

**2:35 – Report and honesty (25 s).** **Cetak laporan**: the A4 report with map, gauges, charts, actions and sources. Every source and capture time is on the last page. "And we say what we have not validated: drought and flood event skill is not testable without field records (README, Validation and limitations). That is our next step with a farmer group in Metro."
