# Brand

## Name

**TUAI** (since 2026-10-31, from the team concept paper): *tuai* means to harvest rice. Short, Indonesian, tied to the end goal (the harvest), and it combines with the feature names: **TUAI Pantau** (monitoring), **TUAI Siaga** (rain and flood alerts), **TUAI Sigap** (disease and pest photos), **TUAI Lancar** (water turns), **TUAI Taksir** (harvest estimate). The chatbot stays **Si Padi**.

The wordmark in the app reads "tuai" in Instrument Serif Italic as live text. The logo files below were regenerated with the "tuai" wordmark on 2026-10-31.

### Earlier name

**Jagapadi** = *jaga* (guard, look after) + *padi* (rice), used until 2026-10-30.

| Candidate | Meaning | Why not chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Jagapadi | guard the rice | chosen |
| Padiloka | rice world | close to "-loka" names already used by other hackathon projects |
| Bulir | grain | hard to extend; common word |
| Sawala | old Javanese for paddy field | unclear to many users |
| Tirta Padi | rice water | long, sounds like a water utility |

Collision check (web search, 2026-10-28): no agriculture product named Jagapadi found. Domain and social-handle availability were not verified by the agent; check before registering.

Earlier sub-brands (retired): Jagapadi Kering, Banjir, Hama, Tanya Padi, Laporan Jagapadi.

## Logo

A rice stalk with a drooping golden panicle and two sage leaves, growing out of paddy water, on a teal felt badge with a stitched edge (the scarf's teal, the mascot's felt). The wordmark is Instrument Serif Italic converted to outlines. Everything is generated by `scripts/brand_assets.py` (2026-10-29, replacing the earlier water-drop mark); edit the script and re-run it rather than editing the files. Files in `frontend/public/logo/`:

- `tuai-lockup.svg` primary (mark + wordmark)
- `tuai-icon.svg` icon, also `favicon.svg`
- `tuai-icon-mono-dark.svg`, `tuai-icon-mono-light.svg`
- `tuai-icon-512.png`, `-192.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `favicon.ico`, `og-image.png`

Legible at 16 px: the badge, the pale stalk and the gold panicle survive at favicon size; `favicon.svg` drops the stitch, which is noise that small. `og-image.png` (1200×630) is the lockup, the tagline and the waving mascot.

## Mascot: Si Padi

Converted from the team's photographs with `scripts/mascot_to_png.py`: the backdrop is fitted as a smooth colour field, alpha comes from Lab chroma distance (shadow excluded), small enclosed holes (eyes, buttons) are filled only if they differ from the backdrop colour, and edge colours are decontaminated. Checked at 400% on dark and light backgrounds. Remaining artefact: a faint peach tint in the narrowest gaps between blades.

Outputs in `frontend/public/mascot/`: `mascot-waving` and `mascot-laptop` at 64-1024 px, `@2x` up to 512, and square avatars (64/128/256). The waving source photo is smaller, so its 1024 px export is upscaled.
