# Research basis

These sources justify the architecture and test design. They do not substitute for
independent project evidence. Links are persistent DOI/publisher records where
available.

## Four primary references

1. Sethy, P. K. et al. (2020), “Deep feature based rice leaf disease identification
   using support vector machine,” *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture* 175,
   105527. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2020.105527>  
   Lineage for the 5,932-image, four-disease source and a warning not to reuse
   lineage data as an independent benchmark.
2. Rahman, C. R. et al. (2020), “Identification and recognition of rice diseases
   and pests using convolutional neural networks,” *Biosystems Engineering* 194,
   112–120. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biosystemseng.2020.03.020>  
   Direct field precedent for distinct disease/pest recognition and compact
   deployment.
3. Petchiammal, C. et al. (2023), “Paddy Doctor: A Visual Image Dataset for
   Automated Paddy Disease Classification and Benchmarking,” CODS-COMAD.
   <https://doi.org/10.1145/3570991.3570994>  
   Field-image taxonomy, expert-assisted annotation, and benchmark design; data
   use remains subject to the exact repository/competition terms.
4. Fenu, G. & Malloci, F. M. (2022), “Evaluating Impacts between Laboratory and
   Field-Collected Datasets for Plant Disease Classification,” *Agronomy* 12, 2359.
   <https://doi.org/10.3390/agronomy12102359>  
   Evidence that controlled performance can collapse in field conditions, which
   motivates the local audit, abstention, and robustness suite.
